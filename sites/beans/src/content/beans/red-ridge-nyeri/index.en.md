Vivid, unmistakably Kenyan acidity recalling blackcurrant and grapefruit, backed by a brown-sugar sweetness that gives the cup real weight.

Although it is a light roast, a four-minute French press brings out more body and shows a different side of the coffee. Quantities are limited this season.
