## Results

| Water | Impression |
|---|---|
| 88 °C | Clear lychee and bergamot, a little thin |
| 92 °C | The best balance of aroma and sweetness |
| 96 °C | More cinnamon, and the ferment becomes prominent |

For pour-over, we suggest starting at 90–92 °C.
