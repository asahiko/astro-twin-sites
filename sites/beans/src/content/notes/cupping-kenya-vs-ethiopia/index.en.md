## Setup

- 12 g coffee, 200 ml water at 93 °C, break at 4 minutes
- Both roasted the same day and cupped five days after roasting

## Impressions

**Red Ridge** has a dense, blackcurrant-and-tomato acidity that turns sweeter as it cools.

**Morning Mist** leads with jasmine and black tea, with a lighter, clearer acidity.

Side by side, the difference reads as "Kenya is fruit you eat, Ethiopia is a flower you smell.
