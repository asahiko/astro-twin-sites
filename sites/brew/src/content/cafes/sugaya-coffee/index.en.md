A roaster-café in the Kasuga area, a little way from Tsukuba Station.

It is often described as roasting specialty-grade beans with an eye to aroma and the bean's own flavor, and people who usually shy away from bitter coffee say they find it easy to drink.

Come here when you want to sit with one good cup rather than a meal, and consider taking some beans home. Please check the shop's own information for opening days and hours.
