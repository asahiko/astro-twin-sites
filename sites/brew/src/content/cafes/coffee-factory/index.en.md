Talk about coffee in Tsukuba for long enough and this name tends to come up.

As you'd expect from a roaster, the place smells of coffee beans, and it has a comfortable feel for anyone who wants to focus on the beans themselves. Enjoy a cup on the spot, or pick up some beans you like and try brewing them at home.

Feel free to ask about the differences between beans; you might discover a new favorite. Please check with the shop for current hours and other details.
