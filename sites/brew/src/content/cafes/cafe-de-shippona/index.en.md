A kissaten in Kitanakajima that has been open since 2000.

Cat-themed décor and a whole wall of teacups give it a warm, slightly nostalgic feel, and its generously filled cream puffs come up often when locals talk about it.

It has the relaxed air of a place the neighborhood has loved for a long time. Please check the shop's own information for hours.
