A coffee roastery in Amakubo, close to the University of Tsukuba.

People come for freshly roasted beans, and the shop is known to host exhibitions of artists' work from time to time, so choosing your coffee can feel a little like visiting a small gallery.

Please check the shop's own information for days off and the latest news.
