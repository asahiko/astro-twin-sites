Saza Coffee was born in Ibaraki and has shops in various places around the prefecture.

Although it's a chain, it clearly takes its coffee seriously, and that's a big part of the appeal. The calm interiors make it easy to settle in and take your time over a cup.

When friends from outside the prefecture ask for "a coffee that feels like Ibaraki," many locals think of this place first. Please check the company's official information for individual shop locations and hours.
