A coffee shop in Kise, set in a converted traditional nagaya-mon gatehouse with a tea room.

Pairing coffee with wagashi, Japanese sweets, is part of the charm here. It suits a slow afternoon away from the bustle around the station.

Weekend and holiday hours are reported to differ from weekdays, so please check the shop's own information before you go.
