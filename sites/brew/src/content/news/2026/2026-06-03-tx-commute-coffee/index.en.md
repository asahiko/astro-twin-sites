The rapid service from Tsukuba Station to Akihabara takes about 45 minutes. When you do it every day, how you spend that time starts to matter.

## A cup before the train

Since Tsukuba is the first stop, you can often get a seat, but eating and drinking on a crowded train still feels awkward. Our suggestion: have your coffee before you leave home, or on the walk to the station. Giving yourself a few unhurried minutes makes a real difference to the morning.

## Hop off one stop early

On the way back, try getting off at Kenkyu-gakuen or Banpaku-kinenkoen and stopping by a café near the station. A quick "detour cup" to switch gears before you get home might just be the TX line's own little ritual.

A thermos of home-brewed drip coffee works too. Find the commute coffee that suits you.
