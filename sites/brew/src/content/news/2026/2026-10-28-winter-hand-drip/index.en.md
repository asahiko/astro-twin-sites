Once the cold winter winds start blowing across the Kanto plain, a home-brewed cup can go lukewarm in no time. Here are a few simple ways to make winter pour-over more enjoyable.

## Warm everything first

Rinse your dripper, server, and cup with hot water before you brew. This alone makes a noticeable difference to the final temperature.

## Use slightly hotter water

Water two or three degrees hotter than in summer helps offset the heat lost during brewing. Too hot can bring out bitterness, though, so adjust as you go.

## Brew less, more often

Making one or two cups at a time, rather than a big batch, means the coffee stays good to the last sip.

Take your time with a steaming cup on those chilly mornings.
